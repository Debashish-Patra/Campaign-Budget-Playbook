Campaign Budget Playbook

How to spend a digital acquisition budget on customers who open accounts and trade, not on cheap enquiries. Four steps, one worked case, and calculators you can change to match your own campaigns.

Worked case: blended CAC ₹16K → ₹12K Measured on activated customers Calculators use illustrative numbers. Overwrite them with yours.
The problem

More spend can buy weaker leads

Cost per lead is the easiest number to improve and the easiest to be fooled by. A campaign that brings in enquiries cheaply can still be the most expensive way to get a customer who opens an account and places a trade.

The fix is to decide what a good outcome is, give the ad platforms that outcome as a signal, and move money towards what produces it.

Optimising for

  • Lowest cost per lead
  • Highest enquiry volume
  • Clicks, form fills, CTR

Optimising for

  • Cost per qualified lead, then cost per activated customer
  • Share of leads that open an account and place a first trade
  • Conversion value and ROAS, once the data is dependable
Worked case

How we cut blended CAC while still scaling

Situation

Digital acquisition for Franchise and Retail ran on a ₹50 Cr budget, with about ₹30 Cr of it in paid media across Google Search, Display and Demand Gen, and Meta. The FY25-26 target was about 1.5 lakh new demat accounts, our share of the company's 4.3 lakh.

What went wrong

When spend was scaled by about 40%, lead quality fell and acquisition cost rose. More money was buying weaker leads.

Diagnosis

We were optimising for cheap enquiries, not for customers who actually opened accounts and traded.

Result

Blended CAC fell from about ₹16K to ₹12K, a reduction of about 25%, while spend kept scaling. It was measured on activated customers, not enquiries, so the saving was real and not a reporting effect.

      Step 2

      Fix the conversion signals

      Ad platforms optimise for whatever conversion you give them. If that is a form fill, they find more form fills. Connect Google Ads (and Meta) to the CRM and to the account-opening and trading events, so the system learns who qualified, who opened and who traded.

      • Capture the click ID (GCLID on Google, the click and browser IDs on Meta) when the lead arrives, and store it on the lead record in the CRM.
      • When the lead status changes in the CRM, back office or trading platform, send that event back to the ad platform against the same click.
      • Report each event under its own name, so you can see cost per qualified lead as well as cost per activated customer.

      Step 3

      Move bidding up in stages

      Each stage needs the one before it to be reliable. Start with target CPA on an event you can trust, then test value-based bidding once conversion values and data quality hold up. Track cost per activated customer, conversion value and ROAS throughout.

      A

      Clean data, no bid change

      Let the new events accumulate under the existing strategy. Compare the platform's numbers with the CRM.

      B

      Target CPA on qualified lead

      Start the target near what you pay today, then tighten in steps as results hold.

      C

      Target CPA on activated customer

      Move to the deepest event with enough volume. Pool low-volume campaigns if you need to.

      D

      Value-based bidding and target ROAS

      Give each activated customer a value, agreed with Finance, and let the system bid for the more valuable ones.

      Step 4

      Reallocate towards quality

      Shift spend towards the campaigns and segments that produce higher-quality customers, not the ones that are merely cheaper per enquiry. In parallel, grow the self-serve (DIY) channel so growth does not depend on relationship-manager capacity.

      • Move budget in steps, then wait for results before the next move.
      • Expect diminishing returns. Extra spend on one campaign usually converts worse than the average.
      • Judge every move on cost per activated customer.

      Glossary

      Terms used here

      Blended CAC

      Total acquisition spend divided by total activated customers, across all channels.

      Activated customer

      A customer who opened an account and placed a first trade.

      Target CPA

      A bid strategy that aims for an average cost per chosen conversion event.

      Target ROAS

      A value-based strategy that aims for a return, as conversion value divided by spend.

      Demand Gen

      Google's campaign type for discovery-style ads across its visual surfaces.

      DIY channel

      Self-serve account opening, with no relationship manager involved.
